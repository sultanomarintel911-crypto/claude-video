# Charsheet Murid SK Sultan Omar (SKeSO)

Pakaian dirujuk daripada **foto sebenar** dalam slaid DUTA 2025 (Microsoft Digital Hub dan SKeSO
Podcast), bukan daripada adegan AI dalam video korporat lama.

> **Penting: wajah murid.** Charsheet ini menjana **watak fiksyen** yang memakai pakaian
> seragam sebenar. Jangan gunakan wajah murid sebenar sebagai rujukan AI tanpa kebenaran
> ibu bapa.

---

## 1. Spesifikasi pakaian seragam

### A. Seragam HARIAN (rujukan: gambar kelas bersama guru, di bawah pokok)
| Item | Lelaki | Perempuan |
|---|---|---|
| Atas | Kemeja **putih lengan pendek**, berkolar, poket dada kiri | **Baju kurung putih** labuh, lengan panjang, longgar hingga paras lutut |
| Lencana | Lencana sekolah **perisai hijau** pada poket dada kiri | Lencana perisai hijau dipin pada tudung, paras dada kiri |
| Tanda nama | Tag nama **kuning/emas** di bawah lencana | Tag nama kuning/emas di bawah lencana pada tudung |
| Bendera | Tampalan kecil **Jalur Gemilang** di dada kanan | Jalur Gemilang kecil pada tudung, sebelah kanan |
| Tali leher | **Hijau tua** dengan lencana sekolah bersulam (tidak semua murid memakainya) | — |
| Bawah | Seluar panjang **biru tua (navy)** | Kain putih / biru sangat muda ⚠️ |
| Tudung | — | **Tudung labuh putih** menutup dada hingga paras pinggang |
| Kasut | Kasut sekolah **hitam** | Kasut sekolah ⚠️ |

### B. Seragam BLAZER (rujukan: foto Microsoft Digital Hub & SKeSO Podcast)
| Item | Lelaki | Perempuan |
|---|---|---|
| Blazer | **Merah marun**, satu baris 2 butang, kolar lapel berlekuk, poket berpenutup | Sama |
| Dalam | Kemeja berkolar terang | Kemeja **biru muda**, tali leher marun |
| Bawah | Seluar panjang biru tua | Kain labuh **biru tua** |
| Tudung | — | **Biru tua (navy)**; sesetengah murid putih |

### C. Baju AKTIVITI
Baju-T **ungu-magenta** dengan tulisan skrip putih **"SKeSO"** di belakang, seluar trek gelap.

⚠️ = belum pasti daripada foto.

## 2. Blok pakaian (salin ke prompt)

**HARIAN_LELAKI**
```
Malaysian primary school boy daily uniform of SK Sultan Omar: crisp white short-sleeve
collared shirt with a left chest pocket, small green shield-shaped school crest badge on the
left pocket, small gold-yellow name tag below the crest, tiny Malaysian flag patch on the right
chest, dark green necktie with an embroidered school crest, long navy-blue trousers, black
school shoes
```

**HARIAN_PEREMPUAN**
```
Malaysian primary school girl daily uniform of SK Sultan Omar: long loose white baju kurung with
long sleeves reaching the knees, long white school tudung (labuh) draping over the chest to
the waist, small green shield-shaped school crest badge pinned on the tudung at the left chest
with a gold-yellow name tag below it, tiny Malaysian flag pin on the right side of the tudung,
long white skirt
```

**BLAZER_LELAKI**
```
SK Sultan Omar blazer uniform: deep maroon (burgundy) single-breasted blazer with two buttons and
notch lapels, flap pockets, light collared shirt underneath, long navy-blue trousers, black shoes
```

**BLAZER_PEREMPUAN**
```
SK Sultan Omar blazer uniform: deep maroon (burgundy) single-breasted blazer with two buttons and
notch lapels, light-blue collared shirt, maroon necktie, long loose navy-blue skirt, navy-blue
school tudung (bawal) covering the chest
```

**AKTIVITI**
```
purple-magenta short-sleeve school activity T-shirt with white handwritten-script "SKeSO"
lettering across the back, dark navy track pants, sports shoes
```

**Bila guna yang mana?**
- **Harian:** dataran perhimpunan, koridor, kelas, PPKI. Ini paling biasa.

## 3. Prompt charsheet (jana setiap satu sebagai helaian rujukan)

Format semua helaian: **latar kelabu neutral**, satu watak, **pandangan depan, sisi ¾,
belakang** + **3 ekspresi wajah** (senyum, fokus, gembira). Pencahayaan studio rata.

**BLOK HELAIAN**
```
character reference sheet, photorealistic, full body turnaround: front view, three-quarter
view, back view, plus three close-up facial expressions (gentle smile, focused, joyful laugh),
plain neutral light-grey studio background, soft even lighting, consistent proportions,
no text, no labels, no watermark
```

### M1 · Murid lelaki Tahap 2 (10–11 tahun)
```
[BLOK HELAIAN] A friendly Malay boy aged 10, short neat black hair, warm brown skin, bright
eyes, wearing [HARIAN_LELAKI]. Second sheet variant: same boy wearing [BLAZER_LELAKI].
```
*Guna untuk: Digital Hub, PK HEM (koridor), PK Pentadbiran (latar).*

### M2 · Murid perempuan Tahap 2 (10–11 tahun)
```
[BLOK HELAIAN] A cheerful Malay girl aged 11, round face, wearing [HARIAN_PEREMPUAN]. Second sheet variant: same girl wearing [BLAZER_PEREMPUAN].
```
*Guna untuk: Podcast, Digital Hub, dataran perhimpunan.*

### M3 · Murid lelaki Tahap 1 (8 tahun)
```
[BLOK HELAIAN] A small Malay boy aged 8, short hair, curious expression, wearing
[HARIAN_LELAKI], shirt slightly loose on his small frame, no necktie.
```
*Guna untuk: PK HEM 3B (membongkok bersama murid kecil).*

### M4 · Murid perempuan Tahap 1 (8 tahun)
```
[BLOK HELAIAN] A small Malay girl aged 8, shy smile, wearing [HARIAN_PEREMPUAN].
```
*Guna untuk: PK HEM 3B, kelas.*

### M5 · Murid sukan / aktiviti (lelaki & perempuan)
```
[BLOK HELAIAN] Two Malay pupils aged 11 standing side by side, a boy and a girl (girl wears
a navy sports tudung), both wearing [AKTIVITI]; back view must clearly show the white
"SKeSO" script on the purple shirts.
```
*Guna untuk: PK Kokurikulum 4A/4B, padang sekolah.*

### M6 · Murid PPKI (pendidikan khas)
```
[BLOK HELAIAN] A happy Malay boy aged 10 with Down syndrome, depicted respectfully and with
dignity, natural features, warm genuine smile, wearing [HARIAN_LELAKI].
```
*Guna untuk: PK Pendidikan Khas 5A/5B. Gambarkan dengan hormat, tiada karikatur.*
